# Responsible Social Media Checklist

### Do's
* **Update Privacy Settings:** Regularly review who can see your posts and personal info.
* **Network Professionally:** Use platforms like LinkedIn to highlight your skills in Python and Data Science.
* **Think Before Posting:** Assume everything you post is permanent and could be seen by a future employer.
* **Fact-Check Content:** Verify information from reliable sources before hitting "Share."
* **Secure Your Accounts:** Enable 2FA to prevent unauthorized access to your identity.

### Don'ts
* **Share Sensitive Info:** Never post your address, student ID, or private contact details.
* **Post During Conflict:** Avoid posting while angry; digital footprints are hard to erase.
* **Plagiarize Content:** Never share others' work or AI-generated content without proper attribution.
* **Ignore Terms of Service:** Be aware of how platforms use your data.
* **Post Compromising Content:** Avoid sharing anything that violates college conduct or professional ethics.
