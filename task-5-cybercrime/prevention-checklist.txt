# Stay Safe Online – Checklist for College Students (India)

## ✔️ General Safety Tips

- Use strong and unique passwords for all accounts (avoid using the same password everywhere).
- Enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) on email, social media, and banking apps.
- Do not click on suspicious links received via email, SMS, or messaging apps.
- Always verify the sender before sharing personal or sensitive information.
- Avoid using public Wi-Fi for logging into important accounts or making transactions.
- Keep your phone, laptop, and apps updated to protect against security vulnerabilities.
- Log out from accounts when using shared or public computers (library, labs, cyber cafés).
- Download apps only from trusted sources like the Google Play Store or Apple App Store.

## 💰 Financial / UPI Safety Tips

- Never share your UPI PIN, OTP, or banking details with anyone—even if they claim to be from the bank.
- Always check the recipient’s name before approving any UPI “Collect Request.”
- Do not scan unknown QR codes, as they may lead to fraudulent payments.
- Avoid saving card or banking details on shared or public devices.

## 🚨 Reporting Cybercrime

- Report cybercrime incidents immediately on the National Cyber Crime Portal: https://cybercrime.gov.in
- Call the Cyber Crime Helpline: **1930** (for financial fraud and urgent help)

---

**Stay alert, think before you click, and protect your digital identity.**
